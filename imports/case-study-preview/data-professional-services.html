Introduction

In an industry where wellness and technology increasingly intersect, a global leader in the weight loss sector sought to modernize and optimize its digital ecosystem. With over 45 years of experience helping millions achieve sustainable health transformations, the company wanted to enhance its diet-tracking and coaching platform across mobile and web applications.

The brand’s mission had evolved alongside the $70 billion fitness industry — from quick fixes and diet trends to data-driven, mindful wellness journeys. To achieve this transformation, the client partnered with NuWare to develop, manage, and scale its end-to-end application infrastructure through a robust DevOps model, ensuring high availability, stability, and superior user experience for millions of global users.

The Challenge

The client required a trusted technology partner capable of owning, managing, and enhancing its digital ecosystem — an integrated diet and wellness platform with web and mobile applications serving thousands of concurrent users.

Key challenges included:

  1. End-to-End Application Management: The client needed a team to manage the existing platform while developing and integrating new features.
  2. High User Load: With over 1 million downloads and an average of 125k web users and 50k mobile users concurrently online, system performance and stability were critical.
  3. Frequent Code Deployments: Continuous feature rollouts and updates were slowing down production and increasing downtime.
  4. Manual and Inconsistent Processes: Legacy deployment workflows caused errors and inefficiencies.
  5. Scalability and Performance Issues: The platform architecture was not optimized for cloud-native scalability or rapid traffic surges.

The client’s goal was to streamline operations, enhance performance, and achieve continuous deployment agility while ensuring uninterrupted user access and data integrity.

NuWare’s Approach

NuWare delivered a comprehensive, multi-tier Professional Services engagement

encompassing DevOps implementation, re-architecture, and 24x7 monitoring and

support. The focus was on creating an automated, secure, and scalable digital foundation capable of handling large user volumes with zero downtime.

1. Cloud Migration and Application Re-Architecture

  • Conducted a detailed assessment of the existing system architecture to identify performance gaps.
  • Executed a smooth and seamless migration to the cloud, enabling elastic scalability and reduced latency.
  • Re-architected the application with enhanced configuration, modularity, and scalability to support continuous feature rollouts without service disruption.

2. DevOps Enablement and Automation

  • Established a CI/CD pipeline for automated, consistent, and fast code deployments across environments.
  • Introduced continuous integration, testing, and deployment practices for faster time-to-market.
  • Automated environment provisioning to reduce manual intervention and human error.

3. Dedicated Delivery and Support Team

  • Deployed a professional, cross-functional team of onsite and offshore engineers skilled in DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and application support.
  • Provided Level 1 and Level 2 monitoring and support to ensure 24x7 operational continuity.
  • Implemented a ticketing and escalation matrix to improve incident management and resolution time.

4. Application Enhancement and Allied Brand Launch

  • Successfully launched a new diet-focused web and mobile application for an allied brand using the same robust infrastructure.
  • Introduced feature enhancements and architecture improvements to increase flexibility and configurability.

5. Process Standardization and Monitoring

  • Standardized all deployment and monitoring processes across teams.
  • Implemented real-time monitoring dashboards for performance tracking and operational visibility.
